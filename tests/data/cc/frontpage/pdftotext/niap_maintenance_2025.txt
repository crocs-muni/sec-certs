Assurance Continuity Maintenance Report:
Leidos submitted an Impact Analysis Report (IAR) and Assurance Continuity Maintenance
package to the Common Criteria Evaluation Validation Scheme (CCEVS) for approval on July 18,
2025 on behalf of Veeam Software. The IAR is intended to satisfy requirements outlined in
Common Criteria Evaluation and Validation Scheme Publication #6, Assurance Continuity:
Guidance for Maintenance and Re-evaluation, version 3.0. In accordance with those requirements,
the IAR describes the changes made to the certified TOE, the evidence updated because of the
changes, and the security impact of the changes.
The evaluation evidence submitted for consideration consists of the Security Target (ST), the
Administrator’s Guides, and the Impact Analysis Report (IAR). The ST and CC Admin Guide were
updated.
